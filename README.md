## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
FarmLink is an AI-powered digital marketplace designed to connect farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers through a single platform.

Farmers can register their products, upload details such as quantity, quality, price, and availability. Consumers and bulk buyers can directly browse available products and place orders without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform also provides logistics support by connecting farmers with transport providers. AI-based demand forecasting helps predict the demand for agricultural products, while route optimization identifies efficient transportation routes.

This creates a transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain where farmers receive better prices and consumers get fresh products at affordable rates.
How it addresses the problem
The current agricultural supply chain often involves multiple intermediaries, transportation delays, price fluctuations, and limited market access for farmers.

FarmLink addresses these issues by:
- Connecting farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Reducing unnecessary intermediaries and commission costs.
- Providing transparent product pricing.
- Helping farmers identify markets with higher demand.
- Connecting sellers with available transportation services.
- Using AI to predict demand and reduce food wastage.
Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
The uniqueness of FarmLink lies in combining a digital agricultural marketplace with AI-powered demand prediction and logistics management.

Unlike a basic buying and selling platform, FarmLink focuses on the entire agricultural supply chain.

Its innovative features include:
- AI-based demand forecasting to help farmers plan their sales.
- Smart price comparison across different markets.
- Route optimization to reduce delivery time and transportation costs.
- Direct bulk-order connections between farmers and businesses.
- Multilingual support to make the platform accessible to rural communities.

## Feasibility and Viability
Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
FarmLink is technically and economically feasible because it can be developed using existing technologies, open-source frameworks, and cloud-based services.

The platform does not require farmers to purchase expensive equipment. They can access it through smartphones or receive assistance from FPOs and local agricultural centres.

The project can initially be implemented in a small geographical area by onboarding a limited number of farmers, buyers, and transport providers. Later, it can be expanded to multiple districts and states.
Potential challenges and risks
- Limited digital literacy among some farmers.
- Unstable internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Difficulty maintaining accurate product availability and quality information.
- Transportation delays and increased delivery costs.
- Inaccurate demand predictions due to seasonal changes.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers.
Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.
- Enable assisted registration through FPOs and agricultural centres.
- Develop a lightweight mobile-friendly application.
- Allow farmers to update product availability easily.
- Regularly update the AI model using new market and sales data.
- Partner with local transport providers to improve delivery reliability.
- Introduce user verification, order records, and feedback mechanisms to build trust

Website: https://sfacindia.com/

4. Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare: Government resources and initiatives related to agricultural development, farmer welfare, and digital agriculture.

Website: https://agriwelfare.gov.in/

These references help establish the need for better market access, transparent pricing, and improved agricultural supply-chain management.
